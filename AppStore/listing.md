## Description
Tatami Day is a private daily calendar for one athlete.

Put every hour of the day on the clock. Divide an hour into 30, 20, 15, or 10 minute blocks. Color each block by what it is: judo, randori, uchikomi, newaza, running, walking, strength, conditioning, mobility, recovery, food, sleep, and the topics you add yourself.

When a session ends, Tatami Day asks you to mark it done. It stays open, and it notifies you, until you do.

Week columns, a month of color, and a simple training load show the work without a spreadsheet.

Tatami Day stays on your iPhone. There is no account on a server. Sign in with Apple is the door, and this copy opens for its registered athlete only.

Tatami Day is a planner. It is not a medical device and it does not tell you how to train or treat an injury.
